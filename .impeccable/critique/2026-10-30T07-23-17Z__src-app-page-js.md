## Design specificity

The design is authored for this person. The globe follows his career route with the projects attached to each port, and the case studies are written as consulting engagements.

The generic parts are the 20-logo technology wall, the GitHub chart, the stat row, the cursor glow, and the 3D gallery barrel, which shows little about the work. The globe also says "ports" while PRODUCT.md says ports are proof, not identity.

**Detector**
- Source scan: one advisory, `codex-grid-background` at `globals.css:131`. False positive: it sits on a map surface.
- URL scan: 22 findings.
  - `ink/60` text is low contrast at 3.4:1, and the testimonial captions are 11px at 3.3:1.
  - Inactive Journey stops fall to 1.5–2.6:1 at 40% opacity.
  - The `numbered-section-labels` hits on Journey 01–06 are a false positive: they mark a real ordered route.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] Proof comes too late.** The first project appears about 3,000px down on desktop and about 4,200px on a phone.
  - Move Advisory, Technology and GitHub below Work.
  - Put the stats and a line naming the flagship work on the first screen.
  - Commands: layout, distill.
- **[P1] The Work barrel is spectacle, not something you can scan.** Its 42 tiles have no labels and spin on their own. On a phone they are 55×31px. Tiles facing away still take keyboard focus.
  - Add a plain project index.
  - Stop the auto-spin once the section settles.
  - Show the filter match count.
  - Make back-facing tiles inert.
  - Use a card row on phones.
  - Commands: distill, adapt.
- **[P1] Phone first screen and reachability.** A third of the first screen is empty. The contact links are 18px tall and the filter chips 26px. There is no nav, and Contact is about 18 screens down. In Journey the text is squeezed and the globe labels are clipped.
  - Command: adapt.
- **[P2] Contrast and focus.**
  - Raise the `ink/60` labels to at least `ink/80`.
  - Make the testimonial captions bigger than 11px.
  - Return focus to the opener when the viewer closes.
  - Hide the commit grid from screen readers.
  - Label the tiles.
  - Commands: audit, harden.
- **[P2] Dead ends and unexplained terms.**
  - "Details →" opens "SCREENS COMING".
  - The site codes and MTPA are not explained.
  - The blurred screens have no framing, which is optional and the user's call.
  - Commands: clarify, harden.
